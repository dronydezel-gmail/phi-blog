Road book · 10–15 November 2026 · home via Bari and Assisi

Siracusa→Riga

Six daylight days home, with two pilgrim stops. A long first day takes you straight to Bari for an evening at the tomb of St Nicholas, and the next afternoon you’re at the tomb of St Francis in Assisi. Then it’s north the usual way through Austria, Czechia, Poland and Lithuania. In both towns you sleep outside the restricted traffic zone, park within a few minutes of the hotel and walk to the church.

Where you sleep

In Bari and Assisi the hotels are just outside the restricted traffic zone (ZTL). You can drive there without passing a camera gate. In Bari you leave the car in the Park Battisti underground car park, 5 minutes from the apartment; in Assisi you park free at the B&B below the town. The walk to the church is 17 minutes in Bari, and 29 minutes in Assisi, going up past Santa Chiara. The other nights are by the motorway.

Set up Waze once

  • Settings → Navigation: allow toll roads and ferries, and don’t avoid highways.
  • Settings → Navigation → Toll passes / Vignettes: add the Austria and Czech Republic vignettes, or Waze may send you off the motorway there.
  • Settings → Gas stations: set the preferred fuel to LPG.

Set up Google Maps once

  • On the route screen, tap ⋯ → Route options and make sure tolls, highways and ferries aren’t avoided.
  • Download offline maps for Italy, Austria, Czechia, Poland and the Baltics, in case you lose signal.

Stage 1 I · St Nicholas

Siracusa → Messina → Sibari → Bari

602 km + ferry≈8 h at the wheelsunrise 06:32sunset 16:39 Bari

The long day. Leave at first light, cross the Strait, and take the toll-free A2 through Calabria to Sibari. From there the E90 runs along the Ionian coast past Trebisacce and Policoro to Metaponto, and you go inland to Bari. You arrive before sunset and walk to the Basilica di San Nicola in the evening; it’s open until about 20:30.

  1. 06:00
    Leave Siracusa with both tanks full. A18 past Catania to Messina.Dawn is 06:04 and sunrise 06:32, so it’s light before Catania.
  2. 08:05
    Messina. Caronte & Tourist to Villa San Giovanni. Buy a ticket at the booth (car ≈€46). Tell the crew you have LPG and drive on using petrol.Cars board at Rada San Francesco: leave the A18 at Messina Boccetta, go straight to the waterfront and follow the Caronte & Tourist signs. Boats leave Messina at 08:00, 08:40 and 09:20; arriving at about 08:05 you’ll make the 08:40 and be off in Villa by about 09:10. Leave Siracusa at 05:50 to catch the 08:00.
  3. 09:05
    North on the A2, toll-free.
  4. 11:15
    Coffee and a GPL top-up near Cosenza.
  5. 12:25
    Sibari: lunch, then the SS106 (E90) along the coast.
  6. 14:15
    Break at Metaponto, then inland past Gioia del Colle to Bari.
  7. 16:05
    Bari, without entering the ZTL. Navigate to Park Battisti, Via Scipione Crisanzio 1/B, the underground car park under Piazza Cesare Battisti, south of the old town. Don’t set the apartment or the Basilica as the destination. Take a ticket and park on level −1: LPG cars aren’t allowed on levels −2 and −3. Then walk 5 minutes to Apartment Andromeda at Via Melo da Bari 205. Don’t drive towards the Castello Svevo or along Corso Vittorio Emanuele.The car park is open 24/7. It costs €2 an hour, capped at €20 for 24 hours; pay at the machines before collecting the car, or by card at the exit barrier.
  8. 16:30
    Walk to the Basilica di San Nicola, 17 minutes from the apartment through the Murat district into Bari Vecchia. Entry is free. The crypt with the saint’s tomb is down the stairs on either side of the altar. Cover shoulders and knees. Afterwards, stay for dinner in Bari Vecchia.Too tired tonight? Go at 07:00 on Wednesday instead and leave Bari around 08:15.

Sleep · Bari, outside the ZTL

  • Booked: Apartment Andromeda, Via Melo da Bari 205. Deluxe double room (25 m²) with its own kitchen, 9.7/10 from 676 reviews. Self check-in 15:00–00:00, and check-out from 01:00, so a 07:30 start is no problem. 17 minutes’ walk to the Basilica. Follow the host’s check-in instructions.
  • Car: Park Battisti, Via Scipione Crisanzio 1/B, under Piazza Cesare Battisti, 5 minutes’ walk from the apartment. Underground, open 24/7, with staff on the intercom around the clock (Interparking/Gestipark, +39 080 990 99 08). It costs €2 an hour, capped at €20 for 24 hours. Park on level −1: the car park’s posted rules bar LPG and methane cars from levels −2 and −3. If level −1 is full, ask on the intercom rather than going lower.
  • Gone: Rosabella Visconti and Il Pumo sold out on 9 Nov. Avoid: Garage Calefati, Via Calefati 406, which bans LPG cars.

Bari ZTL

  • Bari Vecchia is closed to visitors’ cars around the clock. It has camera gates and no visitor permits. The square around the Basilica is pedestrian.
  • A new camera gate opened on 1 July 2026 by the Castello Svevo, between Piazza Federico II di Svevia and Piazza Massari.
  • Fines start around €80 per gate pass and are sent to foreign plates. The apartment and Park Battisti are both outside the zone. The drive to the car park from the south was checked and doesn’t enter the old town.

Fuel and tolls

  • Leave full. The A2 has GPL at several service areas; top up near Cosenza.
  • Keep a quarter tank of petrol for the ferry.
  • Tolls: only the A18 (≈€4). The A2 and SS106 are free.

Heads-up

  • About 10 hours on the road, but all motorway or expressway except the SS106 coastal stretch.
  • Basilica hours vary by source (about 07:00–20:30). Call +39 080 573 7111 to confirm.
  • Puglia food: braciole and ragù are beef or horse. Focaccia, panzerotti and seafood are safe.

Stage 2 I · St Francis

Bari → Pescara → Foligno → Assisi

540 km≈6½ h at the wheelsunrise 06:35sunset 16:50 Assisi

Up the Adriatic on the A14 to Civitanova Marche. Then the SS77, four-lane and toll-free, runs inland through the Apennines to Foligno, and the SS75 brings you to the foot of Assisi. You stay below the town, outside the walls, and walk up past the tomb of St Clare to the tomb of St Francis.

  1. 07:30
    Leave Bari; A14 north. Fill with GPL at the first service area.
  2. 09:50
    Coffee near Termoli.
  3. 12:25
    Lunch near Civitanova Marche, then the SS77 west.
  4. 14:35
    Assisi, without entering the ZTL. Navigate to B&B Cantico delle Creature, Via Cantico delle Creature 5, below the town on the south-east side. Don’t set the Basilica as the destination. Park free at the B&B; check-in is 14:00–20:00.
  5. 15:00
    Walk up to the Basilica di San Francesco, about 30 minutes. After 15 minutes you pass the Basilica di Santa Chiara, with the tomb of St Clare in its crypt and the San Damiano crucifix. Then follow Corso Mazzini, cross the Piazza del Comune and go down Via San Francesco. The tomb of St Francis is in the crypt below the Lower Basilica. See the Upper Basilica first, with Giotto’s frescoes, because it closes earlier in winter (about 17:45).Rather not walk back in the dark? Drive 4 minutes up to the open-air Parcheggio Porta Nuova (paid) instead; it’s 21 minutes on foot from there, and an open-air car park is no problem for LPG.
  6. Evening
    Dinner around the Piazza del Comune on the way back, then about 25 minutes down to the B&B. Sunset is 16:50, so take a torch for the stretch below Porta Nuova.

Sleep · Assisi, outside the ZTL

  • Booked: B&B Cantico delle Creature, Via Cantico delle Creature 5. 9.2/10 from 721 reviews. Continental buffet breakfast, garden and infinity pool. Free private parking on site. Check-in 14:00–20:00, check-out from 07:30.
  • On foot: 15 minutes up to Santa Chiara and 29 minutes to St Francis. Coming back is mostly downhill.
  • Thursday: ask the host whether breakfast can be at 07:00, or packed, so you can still leave at 07:30.

Assisi ZTL

  • The walled centre is a ZTL, with fines of about €92. The B&B and the Porta Nuova car park are outside the walls. The drive to both from the SS75 was checked and doesn’t enter the walled town.
  • The Monastero Sant’Andrea and the B&Bs on Via San Francesco are inside the walls, so they were left out.

St Francis

  • 2026 is the 800th anniversary of his death. The relics were displayed in February and March; check that the crypt is open as usual.
  • Published hours vary: the Lower Basilica is open roughly 06:00–18:30, the Upper Basilica until about 17:45 in winter. Call +39 075 819001.

Fuel and tolls

  • Fill up on the A14, and again near Foligno or Assisi this evening.
  • Tolls: about €30 on the A14. The SS77 and SS75 are free.

Rules of the road

Restricted zones (ZTL)

  • Bari Vecchia and walled Assisi are closed to visitors’ cars, with camera gates and fines from about €80–92.
  • Your satnav doesn’t know about the ZTL. Navigate to the hotel or garage address, never to the church.

Winter tyres

  • LT compulsory from 10 November. You cross on Sunday the 15th.
  • A CZ whenever there’s snow, slush or ice. I from 15 November, after you’ve left. LV from 1 December.

Speed limits

  • I 130, 110 in rain; the SS106 mostly 70–90. A CZ 130. PL 140, expressways 120. LT 110 from November.

In the car

  • Triangle, first-aid kit, vests, and a fire extinguisher (Lithuania and Poland). Dipped headlights on in the daytime.
