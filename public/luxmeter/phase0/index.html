Before you start

Update the app

Your round 2 results are kept: the app moves the old file aside and starts a fresh one. Please don't tap Clear: it would also delete the three photo files I'm asking for at the end.

What you need

  • A dark cloth (a sock or a towel will do)
  • A tape measure
  • The same lamp as last time, at about the iPhone's height, ideally without its shade
  • A few books to lean the iPhone against

What's new in the app

HDR
When a bright light is in view, No clip now takes two or three pictures at different exposures and combines them: the bulb from the short one, the room from the long one. It happens by itself; the screen stays black for up to about 15 seconds, and the result line then says HDR 3 frames.
Linearity sweep
Stops with Too bright for the sweep if the scene is too bright to give exact results.
Camera section
After a sweep it shows a line starting Raw follows ISO up to. The app keeps that number and uses it for the dark and lamp checks, so do check A first.

Leave the switches as they are: "Black screen while measuring" on, "Save DNG files" off.

ASteady response, again Daylight · 5 min

Last time the sweep ran outdoors in sunshine. That's so bright that the main camera had to use shutter speeds of a few hundred-thousandths of a second, where it isn't exact. A room lit through a window is about a hundred times darker, which is what the sweep needs.

Each sweep ends with a line "… linearity · ISO … spread …% · raw stops following ISO above ≈…". No need to check the numbers.

BVery dark, both cameras After dark · 8 min

The same as last time, with the two shots that were missing: a covered main camera, and the ultra wide camera in No clip.

Keep the iPad or laptop screen turned away or face down while capturing: in a dark room it's a light source too. If a covered shot says BURNT OUT, light got in: cover it more firmly and capture again.

COne lamp, two distances After dark · 10 min

Light from a small lamp gets four times weaker when you double the distance. Last time the app could only keep the bulb from burning out by making everything else nearly black, which hid the rest of the room. With HDR it now sees both.

Each result line should mention HDR. If one says BURNT OUT, capture again once; if it still does, carry on.

Send me the results

They show how the light falls off towards the picture corners in two dimensions; the numbers I have so far only give the average by distance from the centre.

If something goes wrong

  • An error in the status line: take a screenshot and send it; carry on with the next check if you can.
  • The app stops responding: close it (swipe up from the bottom and flick it away) and open it again. Captures you've already made are kept.
  • You captured something by mistake: no problem. Type a note on the next capture, for example redo, and repeat the step.
  • Don't tap Clear. It deletes all saved results and photo files.
