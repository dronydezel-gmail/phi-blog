Lux Meter · Phase 0

First camera test on your iPhone 17 Pro

About 40 minutes in total: 20 in daylight, 15 in the evening. No lux meter needed.

What this test is for: before building the real app, we check that your iPhone's camera gives the raw data we need and behaves predictably. The test app shows no lux values yet, only the camera's raw numbers.

You don't need to judge the numbers. Every capture is saved automatically. At the end you send one file and a few screenshots, and I do the analysis.

Tip: keep this page open on your iPad or laptop while you use the iPhone. Tick the boxes as you go; the ticks are remembered on the device you're reading on.

Before you start

Install the test app

What you need

  • The iPhone 17 Pro with the test app
  • One sheet of plain white printer paper
  • A dark cloth (a sock or a towel will do)
  • A tape measure
  • For the evening: one small lamp you can put at about the iPhone's height, such as a table lamp without its shade, or the iPad's torch if it has one
  • Something to lean the iPhone against on a table, such as a few books

Finding your way in the app

It's a plain test screen, from top to bottom:

Preview
What the camera sees.
Status line
The text under the preview. It says what the app is doing, and shows errors.
Ultra wide Main
Chooses which rear camera to use.
Auto Manual
Exposure. Keep it on Auto unless a step says Manual.
Max exposure
Appears in Manual. Sets the longest exposure, for the dark test.
Note
Type the label each step gives you, such as covered. It's saved with the capture.
Capture
Takes one measurement. The screen goes black for a moment; that's on purpose.
Linearity sweep
Takes 8 measurements in a row. Don't move the phone until it finishes.
Export CSV
Sends the saved results. Used at the end.

Leave the switches as they are: "Black screen while measuring" stays on, and "Save DNG files" stays off unless check 3 asks for it.

1Camera details Any time · 3 min

If the status line shows "no Bayer RAW format!" or an error instead, screenshot that and tell me. We'd stop there.

2Steady response Daylight · 5 min

Checks that the camera's numbers stay consistent when it changes shutter speed and sensitivity.

A line "Linearity: … spread …%" appears after each sweep. 10% or less is good, but there's no need to check it.

3Corners vs centre Daylight · 5 min

Shows how much darker the image corners are. This decides how the app combines light from different directions.

4Very dark Evening · 8 min

Checks that the camera can still measure in a room as dark as a bedroom at night, which matters most for the bedtime readings.

In the dark each capture can take a few seconds. Wait for the screen to come back.

5One lamp, two distances Evening · 7 min

Light from a small lamp gets four times weaker when you double the distance. This checks the camera sees exactly that.

Send me the results

If something goes wrong

  • An error in the status line: take a screenshot and send it; carry on with the next check if you can.
  • The app stops responding: close it (swipe up from the bottom and flick it away) and open it again. Captures you've already made are kept.
  • You captured something by mistake: no problem. Just type a note on the next capture, for example redo, and repeat the step.
  • Don't tap Clear. It deletes all saved results.
