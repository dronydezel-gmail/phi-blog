EyeLux · Phase 0 · Round 2

Second camera test on your iPhone 17 Pro

About 30 minutes: 10 in daylight, 20 after dark. Still no lux meter needed.

Thanks for round 1. The camera details were all good, and the dark test worked well on the main camera: it clearly told a very dim room from a covered lens.

Two things went wrong on my side. The test app misread your iPhone's raw numbers (they come on a different scale from the one the photo file states), and my lamp instructions said Auto, which lets the lamp burn out to white in the picture. Both are fixed in the new version. This round repeats only what's still missing.

Tip: keep this page open on your iPad or laptop while you use the iPhone. Tick the boxes as you go; the ticks are remembered on the device you're reading on.

Before you start

Update the app

Your round 1 results are kept: with the first new capture the app moves the old file aside and starts a fresh one. No need to tap Clear.

What you need

  • One sheet of plain white printer paper
  • A dark cloth (a sock or a towel will do)
  • A tape measure
  • The same lamp as last time, at about the iPhone's height, ideally without its shade
  • A few books to lean the iPhone against

What's new in the app

Auto No clip Manual
Exposure now has three choices. No clip is new and is now the default: the app picks the exposure itself, as bright as it can without any part of the picture burning out to white. It sometimes takes a few shots in a row, so the screen can stay black for several seconds.
Linearity sweep
Now takes about 15 shots, at every sensitivity the camera offers. Takes up to a minute.
BURNT OUT
If a result line shows this, part of the picture was too bright. You don't need to do anything; it tells me which captures to ignore.

Leave the switches as they are: "Black screen while measuring" stays on, and "Save DNG files" stays off unless check B asks for it.

ASteady response Daylight · 5 min

Checks that the camera's numbers stay consistent across shutter speeds and sensitivities, including the very high sensitivity it uses in the dark. Last time the sweep results didn't make it into the file.

A line "… linearity · ISO … spread …%" appears after each sweep. It may also say "raw stops following ISO above …": that's a measurement of your camera, not a fault. No need to check any of it.

BCorners vs centre Daylight · 5 min

Shows how much darker the image corners are. This decides how the app combines light from different directions.

CVery dark, both cameras After dark · 8 min

Last time the ultra wide camera only got one shot in the dark. This time both cameras look at the same dim scene, so I can compare them directly.

Keep the iPad or laptop screen turned away or face down while capturing: in a dark room it's a light source too.

DOne lamp, two distances After dark · 10 min

Light from a small lamp gets four times weaker when you double the distance. This checks the camera sees exactly that. Last time it measured only about 2× weaker, mostly because Auto let the lamp burn out to white.

If a result line says BURNT OUT, capture again once. If it still says so, carry on; it's useful information too.

Send me the results

No screenshots needed this time, unless something shows an error.

If something goes wrong

  • An error in the status line: take a screenshot and send it; carry on with the next check if you can.
  • The app stops responding: close it (swipe up from the bottom and flick it away) and open it again. Captures you've already made are kept.
  • You captured something by mistake: no problem. Just type a note on the next capture, for example redo, and repeat the step.
  • Don't tap Clear. It deletes all saved results.
